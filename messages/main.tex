\section{Network}

\subsection{Transport}

\input{messages/uml_webrtc}

One, and only one, \texttt{WebRTCManager} MUST be used for one, and only one, WebRTC session. Once the WebRTC session has been created, after the exchange of offer, response to offer, and response to response to offer, both peers MUST exchange their public keys, OTK, and pre-keys (X3DH). Then, \texttt{Session} is created. 
\bigskip

Transport MUST operate over \nameref{sec:dtls}.

\section{Events}

\input{messages/uml_events}

\texttt{Event} MAY be encrypted, depending if session is initialized. When \texttt{Event} is \texttt{Message(Message)} or \texttt{Typing()}, \texttt{Event} MUST be encrypted.
